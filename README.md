# JamAcademy2026

## PRESSION SOUS-PRESSION

*Bajama is a fish man, and they and their child Pakal (who is a fish), suffer a great loss after the parent's divorce...*

But that's when Bajama has an idea: What if they made some money to buy a ring to get their lover back, and be a happy family again! With their strong arms, they build a submarine, make it a bar where all the divers can get a taste of his delicious beer, and enjoy Pakal's company ! 

Embark on a journey with a man who only wants to get their life back by serving some fresh beers! Get all the orders right, get the customers satified, and try to get Pakal to survive! You will have to serve the beers in time, manage to pay the rent in time, get the orders right, and there are also some hidden mechanics that you can try and find. It will get harder as you go on during the week, and you will need to be vigilent about what you do with the drinks or else the clients will leave and you might not be able to pay the rent...

Experience a bar managing gameplay experience with a story to be discovered, upgrades and 6 different endings !

This game was created as part of the JamAcademy 2026, for the theme 'Under Pressure'. It fits it by happening in a submarine, so there's water pressure, beer pressure from the taps, and the pressure of stress the game puts the player under! 

The game is played with the mouse using the left click and the Esc key for the pause menu. 


For this game we were a team of 7 people, all working from 42_Marseille :

- Jolyne, Benjamin, Pascal and Camille were on back-end programming

- Sarhynn was our artist/game artist

- Emma, Ady and Pascal worked both on narration and sound design and integration

We still all participated in the global conception, imagination of the gameplay and visuals, and always worked together !

No AI was used to generate any code nor asset for this project.
